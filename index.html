Graph Theory · Mackenzie

Brazilian air network

How connected are Brazil’s airports, and what are the shortest routes between them? I built a weighted graph from OpenFlights data in Python and ran graph search, connectivity analysis, Dijkstra, Floyd–Warshall and Kruskal on it. Here is a smaller version you can play with.

Tip: click two airports on the map.

Kruskal sorts every route by distance and keeps the shortest one that doesn’t close a loop, until every airport is connected.

Floyd–Warshall finds the shortest distance between every pair of airports. The best-placed hub has the lowest average distance to everyone else.

    Simplified for the browser: 26 airports, illustrative routes, great-circle distances.
